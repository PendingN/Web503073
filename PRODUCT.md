# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Vũ Điệu Rừng Xanh is a Vietnamese plant reference and community website. Visitors browse plants and care notes, save favorites after signing in, and read or contribute blog posts. It does not sell plants.

## Users

The landing-page brief addresses people interested in indoor plants and home decor. The user wants visitors to find plants that suit their own room.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing server-rendered PHP 8.0+, MySQL, plain public CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
- Preserve the catalog, account, favorite, blog, and admin workflows.
- No new dependencies, database migrations, or invented commercial claims are needed for the landing page.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the name Vũ Điệu Rừng Xanh and Vietnamese public copy. The user requests an indoor-plant/decor landing page with a room corner at the top and effects that encourage exploration. The confirmed visual preference is minimal, bright, airy, with few objects.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing catalog includes indoor plants and care notes (`data/plants.php`); local plant images are in `images/` and `images/optimized/`. No customer testimonials, sales statistics, or commercial services have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Let visitors see plants in a room before reading more.
- Help visitors start from their actual light and available space.
- Offer a few editorial suggestions and link to full catalog care notes.
